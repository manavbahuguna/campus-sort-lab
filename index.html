THE COURSE CONNECTION

Follow the waste stream

UNIT III

Collect

Segregated waste goes into labelled stationary bins. Collection vehicles empty the bins, and recyclables can go to a material recovery facility for sorting.

UNIT IV

Process

Balers compact cardboard for transport. Shredders reduce accepted plastics in size. Facilities can separate metals using magnets and eddy currents.

UNIT V

Dispose

Where residual waste goes to a sanitary landfill, liners limit seepage, leachate drains collect contaminated liquid, and gas systems collect landfill gas.

These are example pathways. Follow campus signage for actual collection rules.
